## Scope acknowledgement

The representative signing below acknowledges that the system, as currently built and
demonstrated, does **not** include: automatic record disposal (the system notifies when a
record is due for retention review; disposal remains a manual office process), AI- or
machine-learning-based recommendations (priority suggestions are a fixed rule based on due
date), attendance tracking (Time Logs record sign-in/out for the system itself, not office
attendance), and upload/storage of records' source documents (the system manages the
masterlist entry — code, title, medium, location, period covered, filing system, access
control, retention period — not the document file itself).
