Level 1 — System Decomposition

Process 0 broken into its five major processes, each with its own data store. Every request/response pair is a separate single-direction arrow; no process connects directly to another process — task and records events reach Send Notifications only through D4.

Level 2 — Decomposition of Process 2.0 (Manage Tasks)

Breaks Manage Tasks into its five sub-processes. All five still read and write the same D2 Tasks store shown at Level 1; 2.5 is the sub-process responsible for writing the pending-event records into D4.

Sub-process descriptions

IDSub-processDescription
2.1Create & Assign TaskCreates a task record from a Super Admin directive or Admin assignment, including assignee(s), due date, priority, and attachments; writes it to D2.
2.2Update Task ProgressAccepts an employee's status/progress update, writes it to D2, and notifies the responsible Admin.
2.3Submit Proof of CompletionAccepts an uploaded proof file from the employee, writes the submission to D2, and flags the task "For Review" to the Admin.
2.4Review & Validate SubmissionAccepts the Admin's approve/return decision, writes the validation result to D2, and notifies the employee of the outcome.
2.5Monitor & Flag Due TasksReads D2 on a schedule to find tasks approaching or past their due date, and writes a pending-event record to D4 for each one.

Level 2 — Decomposition of Process 3.0 (Manage Records)

Breaks Manage Records into its four sub-processes, all reading and writing the same D3 Records store shown at Level 1.

Sub-process descriptions

IDSub-processDescription
3.1Log & Classify RecordAccepts a new record entry (title, reference number, category, retention period) from Admin or Employee and writes it to D3.
3.2Search & Retrieve RecordsAccepts a search query, reads matching records from D3, and returns the masterlist results.
3.3Monitor Retention & DisposalReads D3 on a schedule to find records approaching or past their retention review date, and writes a pending-event record to D4.
3.4Archive / Dispose RecordAccepts the Admin's retain/archive/dispose decision, updates the record's status and archival reference in D3, and confirms the action back to the Admin.
